Classmoji grades with a scale you choose: expressive emoji, each mapped to a number, or a plain numeric scale. Either way, every grade on a submission is a number underneath, so weights and averages just work.

### Grading scales

Set up your scale in **Settings > Grades**.

**Emoji scale**: add emoji and give each one a numeric value (0–100). **Populate Defaults** starts you with a sensible set. A submission's grade is the mean of the emoji given to it; if several graders grade the same submission, their grades average.

**Numeric scale**: click **Populate 0–100 scale** to get the built-in scores, `score-0` through `score-100` in steps of 5. Graders then type a score instead of picking an emoji.

### Grading an assignment

Open a module, click an assignment, and you're on its [assignment page](/docs/instructors/modules-and-assignments#the-assignment-page). Each row is one submission: a student (or team), their repo, and its status.

<Screenshot src={gradingImg} alt="An assignment page with per-student submission rows, grades, graders, and the release toggle" frame url="app.classmoji.io/admin/cs-101/assignments/lab-1" size="lg" />

- **Emoji scale**: click **Grade** on the row to open the emoji picker, then click an emoji to add it. Click the same emoji again to remove it.
- **Numeric scale**: the **Grade** column holds an inline number field. Type a value from the scale and press **Tab** or **Enter** to save. Values that aren't on the scale are refused. Each grader has one score per submission; entering a new one replaces your previous score, while other graders' scores stay and average with yours.

Filters (**All / Ungraded / Late / Not submitted**) and the **Submitted / Late / Graded / Ungraded** tiles keep track of where you are.

### Graders

Use **Assign graders** on the assignment page to split a large class between staff. In a row, the **Graders** column lists who's assigned; **Assign** or **Change** opens a checklist of staff flagged as graders. Assistants get a grading queue that gathers every submission assigned to them across the class.

<Screenshot src={gradingQueueImg} alt="The assistant grading queue listing submissions by owner, repository, and assignment with status tabs" frame url="app.classmoji.io/assistant/cs-101/grading" size="lg" />

### Releasing grades

Grades are hidden from students by default. Each assignment page has a **Grades released** switch. Flip it and students see their grade immediately. Releasing is per assignment, so you can release one while keeping others hidden.

### Late penalties

Set **Late penalty points per hour** in **Settings > Grades**. A submission that lands after the deadline (plus any extension the student bought with tokens) is docked that many points per late hour. To forgive a specific submission, open the **⋮** on its row and choose **Waive late penalty**; **Restore late penalty** undoes it.

### Grade calculations

<GradeCalculationDiagram />

**Submission grade**: the mean of the grades given to that submission (emoji values or scores, across graders), minus any late penalty.

**Course grade**: the weighted mean of every graded, non-extra-credit assignment, using each assignment's weight, plus any extra-credit assignments on top. Repositories carry no weight, and there is no drop-lowest.

### Letter grades

Map score ranges to letters in **Settings > Grades** (for example, 90–100 = A, 80–89 = B). The gradebook's **Breakpoints** popover shows the same cutoffs. A student's letter can be overridden on their [report](#student-report).

### Gradebook

**Grades** (owner and teacher) is the full gradebook: one row per student, one column per published assignment.

<Screenshot src={gradesImg} alt="The gradebook showing students with letter grades, raw grades, and final grade columns" frame url="app.classmoji.io/admin/cs-101/grades" size="lg" />

- Search students, and use **Show** to filter: **Everyone**, **Has something to grade**, **Has a missing submission**, **Late somewhere**.
- Columns are grouped by type, **Repositories**, **Quizzes**, **Forms**, in deadline order. Each header shows the title (linked to the assignment page), its module, weight, due date, and how many are left to grade.
- Cells show the score, or **To grade** (blue), **Late** (amber), **Missing** (red), or **Not submitted**. Quiz cells show the score, **In progress**, or **Not attempted**; form cells **Responded** or **No response**.
- Grade from the cell: type a score on a numeric scale, or click **Grade** to open the emoji picker on an emoji scale. Clicking an existing score jumps to that student's row on the assignment page.
- **Total** (the weighted score; hover for the before-late-penalty and individual-only figures) and **Letter** (from the breakpoints, amber when overridden) stay pinned on the right. A class row at the bottom shows the mean and median.

### Student report

Click the eye icon on a gradebook row (or **View** on the Students list) to open a student's report at `/admin/:class/students/:login` (also under `/teacher`).

It shows the student's name, login, school ID (owner-editable), and tokens; tiles for **Total** with letter, **Graded so far**, **Late** (hours and penalty), and **Final letter override**; then their assignments grouped into **Repositories**, **Quizzes**, and **Forms**, each with its module, status, grade, **View** (Github), **Waive late**, and **Grade now**. A **Private note** at the bottom is visible to staff only.
