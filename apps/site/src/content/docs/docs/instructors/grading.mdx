Classmoji grades with a scale you choose: expressive emoji, each mapped to a number, or a plain numeric scale. Either way, every grade on a submission is a number underneath, so weights and averages just work.

### Grading scales

Set up your scale in **Settings > Grades**.

**Emoji scale**: add emoji and give each one a numeric value (0–100). Pick **Emoji scale** from the **Populate template** menu to start with 🚀 100, 👍 90, 👀 80, 👎 60 and 😭 0. A submission's grade is the mean of the emoji given to it; if several graders grade the same submission, their grades average.

**Numeric scale**: pick **Number scale** from the **Populate template** menu to get the built-in scores, `score-0` through `score-100` in steps of 10. Graders pick a score badge from the same hover picker used for emoji, and each grader holds one score at a time: picking another replaces it.

### Grading an assignment

Open a module, click an assignment, and you're on its [assignment page](/docs/instructors/modules-and-assignments#the-assignment-page). Each row is one submission: a student (or team), their repo, and its status.

<Screenshot src={gradingImg} alt="An assignment page with per-student submission rows, grades, graders, and the release toggle" frame url="app.classmoji.io/admin/cs-101/assignments/lab-1" size="lg" />

- **Emoji scale**: click **Grade** on the row to open the emoji picker, then click an emoji to add it. Click the same emoji again to remove it.
- **Numeric scale**: the **Grade** column holds an inline number field. Type a value from the scale and press **Tab** or **Enter** to save. Values that aren't on the scale are refused. Each grader has one score per submission; entering a new one replaces your previous score, while other graders' scores stay and average with yours.

Filters (**All / Ungraded / Late / Not submitted**) and the **Submitted / Late / Graded / Ungraded** tiles keep track of where you are.

### Graders

Use **Assign graders** on the assignment page to split a large class between staff. In a row, the **Graders** column lists who's assigned; **Assign** or **Change** opens a checklist of staff flagged as graders. Assistants get a grading queue that gathers every submission assigned to them across the class.

<Screenshot src={gradingQueueImg} alt="The assistant grading queue listing submissions by owner, repository, and assignment with status tabs" frame url="app.classmoji.io/assistant/cs-101/grading" size="lg" />

### Releasing grades

Grades are hidden from students by default. Each assignment page has a **Grades released** switch. Flip it and students see their grade immediately. Releasing is per assignment, so you can release one while keeping others hidden.

### Late penalties

Set **Late penalty points per hour** in **Settings > Grades**. A submission that lands after the deadline (plus any extension the student bought with tokens) is docked that many points per whole late hour. To forgive a specific submission, open the **⋮** on its row and choose **Waive late penalty**; **Restore late penalty** undoes it.

Quizzes follow the same rule: an attempt finished after the due date (plus the hours the student bought) loses that many points per whole late hour, and the quiz's grading strategy (highest, most recent, or first attempt) picks among the penalised scores. There is no waive for quizzes; a student can buy the late hours out with tokens.

### Grade calculations

<GradeCalculationDiagram />

**Repository assignment grade**: the mean of the grades given to the submission (emoji values or scores, across graders), minus any late penalty. A submission still missing after the deadline (plus bought hours) counts as 0.

**Quiz assignment grade**: the score of the attempt picked by the quiz's grading strategy, minus any late penalty. It counts as soon as the attempt is scored. A student with no finished attempt once the due date (plus the hours they bought) has passed gets a 0; a quiz with no due date never counts as 0. Students see their own score before the penalty, with the late hours beside it; staff totals use the penalised score.

**Course grade**: Σ(grade × weight) / Σ weight over every graded, non-extra-credit assignment, then each graded extra-credit assignment adds grade × weight / 100. Weights are relative, so they don't need to add up to 100. Repositories carry no weight, forms carry no grade, and there is no drop-lowest. Quizzes count only where quizzes are turned on for the classroom.

### Letter grades

Map score ranges to letters in **Settings > Grades** (for example, 90–100 = A, 80–89 = B). The gradebook's **Breakpoints** popover shows the same cutoffs. A student's letter can be overridden on their [report](#student-report).

### Gradebook

**Grades** (owner and teacher) is the full gradebook: one row per student, one column per published assignment.

<Screenshot src={gradesImg} alt="The gradebook showing students with letter grades, raw grades, and final grade columns" frame url="app.classmoji.io/admin/cs-101/grades" size="lg" />

- Search students, and use **Show** to filter: **Everyone**, **Has something to grade**, **Has a missing submission**, **Late somewhere**.
- Columns are grouped by module, in course order, each module ending with its **Module total** (collapse a module to show only its total). Each header shows the title (linked to the assignment page), weight, due date, and how many are left to grade.
- Cells show the score, or **To grade** (blue), **Late** (amber), **Missing** (red), or **Not submitted**. Quiz cells show the score the total counts (after any late penalty, amber with the late hours when late), **0 (not attempted)** (red) once the due date has passed with no finished attempt, or else **In progress** or **Not attempted**; form cells **Responded** or **No response**.
- **Has a missing submission** and **Late somewhere** include quizzes.
- Grade from the cell: hover **Grade** to open the picker (emoji, or score badges on a numeric scale). Clicking an existing score jumps to that student's row on the assignment page.
- **Total** (the weighted score; hover for the before-late-penalty and individual-only figures) and **Letter** (from the breakpoints, amber when overridden) stay pinned on the right. A class row at the bottom shows the mean and median.

### Student report

Click the eye icon on a gradebook row (or **View** on the Students list) to open a student's report at `/admin/:class/students/:login` (also under `/teacher`).

It shows the student's name, login, school ID (owner-editable), and tokens; tiles for **Total** with letter, **Graded so far**, **Late** (hours and penalty), and **Final letter override**, each counting repository and quiz assignments; then their assignments grouped by module, each with its status, grade, **View** (Github), **Waive late**, and **Grade now**. A quiz row shows the score the total counts, its late hours, or **Not attempted** with a 0 once the due date has passed. A **Private note** at the bottom is visible to staff only.
